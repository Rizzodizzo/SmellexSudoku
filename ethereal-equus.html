    How to play

    6 + 7 = ?
    Live preview: the road on top, your question and answers underneath. Pick the right one to speed up.
    The goalRace 2,500 m and cross the line ahead of three bots. Finish in the top 3 to earn jellybeans, then open packs in the Garage.
    Answer to go fastA right answer adds speed and fuel. A wrong one spins you out, drains fuel and slows you down. No fuel and you roll to a stop. Tap a choice or press 1-4.
    SteeringA / D or the arrow keys, or tap the left or right side of the road.
    On the roadYellow arrow pads give a speed boost. Blue ramps launch you over traffic after a quick Up / Down question. The unicorn gives a true or false for a magic boost and a rainbow shield. Tuck in right behind a bot for a slipstream.
    History leapEvery 5th question is history. Get it right to leap forward over the cars.
    School zoneSlow and steady. Hitting a kid costs 15 fuel, and one wrong answer in the zone ends your race.
    Airborne modeFly a jet over the ocean against the clock and get as far as you can. Type each sentence exactly to build speed, and every finished sentence adds 3 seconds to the clock. Wrong letters turn red and must be fixed before you move on.
    Storms and the pegasusThe jet hovers by itself while you type. Finishing a sentence gives a huge afterburner boost. In the last 20 seconds a thunderstorm hits and skydivers rain down for 5 seconds: click a parachute to cut it loose, because crashing into a diver halves your speed and costs 1.5 seconds. Catch the pegasus with a click and answer its calorie question for a big boost and 3 extra seconds. Press Tab to jump back to the typing box.
